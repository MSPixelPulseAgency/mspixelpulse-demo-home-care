# Content Agent

Maintain warm, reassuring, plain-English copy for seniors, adult children, spouses and family caregivers. The configured brand is Serenity Home Care with the tagline “Compassionate Care. Right at Home.” Placeholder contacts live in `src/config/siteConfig.js` and must never be manually repeated with different values.

Treat all services as non-medical assistance. Valid language includes support, companionship, reminders, routines, personal comfort, family communication and independence. Do not claim nursing, therapy, diagnosis, medication administration or clinical monitoring. Never invent licences, credentials, staffing policies, response times, guarantees, prices, statistics or client stories.

Keep testimonials labeled as sample content until written client approval is recorded. Resource articles must remain general education and must not become medical or legal advice. Privacy and terms pages are starter structure requiring final legal review.

SEO copy should naturally mention Dayton, Ohio where relevant without keyword stuffing. Every page needs one clear H1, logical headings, a useful title and description, internal links and an appropriate CTA. Preserve the MSPixelPulse footer credit and demo notice.
